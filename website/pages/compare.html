FAQ

Choosing school software

How is Smart Gurukul different from other school management software?

Most school management software is a record-keeping tool for the office. Smart Gurukul covers the core records (students, staff, attendance, fees, payroll, exams, report cards and the timetable) and adds AI tools, GPS teacher attendance with no biometric machine, school bus tracking from the driver’s phone, and a game layer that gives students a reason to open the app every day.

Is Smart Gurukul a good fit for a small or mid-sized private school?

Yes. It’s made for private CBSE and State Board schools in India of any size. Each school, or each branch of a group, runs as its own Smart Gurukul account. It needs no hardware, our team imports your existing register, and you can try it free for a month.

What does Smart Gurukul not do yet?

Online fee payment, homework, SMS, hostel, library, statutory payroll (PF/ESI/TDS), full accounting and automatic timetable generation are not available.

Can we move from our current software to Smart Gurukul?

Yes. Export your students and staff to Excel, or share the register you keep, and our team imports it. You can run the free 1-month pilot alongside your current system.
