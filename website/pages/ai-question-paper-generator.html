Tell the AI the class, subject and chapters. It drafts the questions, answers and marks, and you check everything before the paper goes anywhere.

Book a free demo

In short: a teacher picks one of their classes and a subject, writes the chapters or topics to cover, and sets the paper type (quiz, test, exam or assignment check), difficulty (easy, medium, hard or mixed), number of questions (up to 30) and total marks. The AI drafts the paper with an answer, explanation and marks for every question. The teacher checks it, shares it as a PDF with or without the answer key, and can save the multiple-choice questions to the school’s question bank for Battle Rooms and the Arena.

What can a teacher choose?

Everything a paper needs, in a single form. The AI drafts from exactly what the teacher fills in.

  • Class and subject: teachers pick from the classes they teach; principals and admins can pick any class.
  • Chapters or topics: in the teacher’s own words, such as “Light: reflection and refraction”.
  • Type: quiz, test, exam or assignment check.
  • Difficulty: easy, medium, hard or mixed.
  • Size: up to 30 questions, and the total marks for the paper.
  • Question formats: multiple choice, true/false, one-word, numeric, short answer, long answer, or a mix.
  • Extra instructions: anything else the paper should follow.

FAQ

AI question papers: common questions

Is the AI paper ready to use as it is?

No. It is a draft. Check every question, answer and mark before you use it in class.

Which question types can it make?

Multiple choice, true/false, one-word, numeric, short answer and long answer questions, or a mix of them in one paper.

How many questions can one paper have?

Up to 30, with the total marks you set split across them.

Can students use it to get answers?

No. The question paper generator is for teachers and admins. Students have Academic Helper, which teaches them the method instead of giving away answers.

Can I ask for my board’s pattern?

Describe the pattern you want in the extra instructions. The AI drafts from what you give it, and you check the result.
