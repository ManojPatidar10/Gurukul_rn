AI for schools that works on your school’s own data

Two AI assistants are built into the Smart Gurukul app. One answers questions about your school from live records. The other helps teachers teach and students learn. Both understand English, Hindi and Hinglish.

Book a free demo

In short: Smart Gurukul’s AI Helpdesk lets a principal or admin ask things like “how many students haven’t paid fees?” and get the answer from the school’s real data, while the Academic Helper writes teaching notes and lesson plans for teachers and explains doubts to students. Both are part of the app, not a separate paid add-on.

AI Helpdesk

Ask your school a question. Get a number, not a spreadsheet.

The Helpdesk is a chat assistant in the app. For admins it can look up live school records, so questions that used to mean calling the accountant get answered in one message.

  • Fees: “How many students still owe fees?” “What is outstanding for Class 9-B?”
  • Attendance: “What is Sunita ma’am’s attendance percentage this month?”
  • Payroll: “Whose salary hasn’t been paid for August?”
  • Enrolment: “How many students are enrolled in Class 6?”

It replies in the language you asked in: English, Hindi or Hinglish. Beyond data questions it also helps with general tasks like drafting a notice or planning a lesson.

Academic Helper

A teaching assistant for every teacher, a tutor for every student

Teachers get prep work done faster. Students get a patient explanation any time they’re stuck.

  • For teachers: teaching notes, lesson plans, learning objectives and practice questions, following CBSE/NCERT or your board’s pattern.
  • For students: step-by-step explanations of doubts in any subject, at their class level.
  • Role-aware: it answers a teacher as a colleague and a student as a tutor.

Built safely

How the AI handles your school’s data

🔒

No free-form database access

The Helpdesk doesn’t write its own database queries. It can only call a fixed set of vetted lookups, each locked to your school.

🧑‍💼

Data lookups are admin-only

School-wide numbers like fee dues and payroll are only available to admin accounts.

🏫

Your school only

Every lookup is scoped to the school you’re signed into. One school can never see another’s records.

🗣️

Your language

Ask in English, Hindi or Hinglish; the answer comes back the same way.

FAQ

AI in school: common questions

What can AI do for a school in India?

In Smart Gurukul, AI answers admin questions from live school data (fee dues, attendance percentages, unpaid salaries, enrolment), helps teachers prepare teaching notes and lesson plans, and tutors students through their doubts, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

Does the AI Helpdesk understand Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes. It replies in whichever language the question was asked in: English, Hindi or Hinglish.

Can the AI see other schools’ data?

No. Every lookup is locked to the school you are signed into, and the AI can only use a fixed set of vetted lookups rather than writing its own database queries.

Is the AI a separate paid add-on?

No. The AI Helpdesk and the Academic Helper are part of the Smart Gurukul app.

Does the Academic Helper follow the CBSE syllabus?

It follows CBSE/NCERT by default when you ask for teaching material, and you can ask it to follow your State Board’s pattern instead. Teachers should always review generated material before using it in class.
