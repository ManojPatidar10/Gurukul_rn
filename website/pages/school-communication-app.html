Chat and school notices in one app, instead of WhatsApp groups

Staff message each other and students, and parents message their child’s teachers and the school office, one to one in the school’s own app. The school sends notices to everyone, one class or one grade, straight to their phones.

In short: Smart Gurukul has one-to-one chat and school notices. Staff can message each other and students, and students can message any staff member. Students can’t message each other, and there are no group chats. A parent with a parent account can message their child’s class teacher, subject teachers and the school’s admins in their own name. A message can carry a photo or a PDF of up to 10 MB, and the other person gets a push notification. For notices, the principal or an admin posts to the whole school, and a class teacher posts to their own class or their whole grade, from School Events: a written notice with “Post a notice”, or an event with an RSVP, a registration form or a poll. All staff, the students it covers and their parents’ accounts get a push, and parent accounts keep a list of the school’s notices. There’s no calling or SMS in the app.

Notices

How do school notices work?

Notices are posted from School Events. “Post a notice” sends a written notice, and an event can also ask families to reply.

  1. Open “School Events” and tap “Post a notice”. The form is in Hindi and English.
  2. Pick who it’s for: “Whole school” (admin), “One section” (admin or that section’s class teacher) or “All sections of a class” (admin or a class teacher in that class). The app shows only the choices you’re allowed.
  3. Write a title and the message, and tap “Send”. The app asks once more and says who will be notified, because a notice can’t be edited or deleted after it is sent.
  4. All staff, the students it covers and their parents’ accounts get a push within seconds.
  5. Parents with a parent account find it under “Announcements”.

To ask families to reply, create an event in School Events with an RSVP, a registration form or a poll. Staff and students see it there and can RSVP, register or vote, and everyone it covers gets a notice about it. An event saved as “Just an announcement” sends a notice too, titled with the event’s name.

What arrives on the phone?

Each new message and each notice arrives as a push notification. On Android they come on separate notification channels, “Messages” and “Announcements”, so people can mute one without losing the other.

FAQ

Chat and notices: common questions

Can parents message teachers?

Yes, with a parent account. A parent messages their child’s class teacher, subject teachers and the school’s admins, one to one and in their own name. A parent account is created in the app and approved by the school.

Can students chat with each other?

No. Students can message staff, but not each other, and there are no group chats.

How do we send a notice to one class?

Open School Events, tap “Post a notice”, choose “One section” and the section, write the notice and send it. The admin can post to any section, and a class teacher to their own section or every section of their class. All staff, the students in that section and their parents’ accounts get a push.

Can we send photos or documents?

Yes. A chat message can carry a photo or a PDF of up to 10 MB.

Do parents need a parent account to get notices?

The push goes to the students a notice covers and to their parents’ accounts, so a parent signed in on the child’s account with the WhatsApp code gets it too. Only a parent account keeps a list of past notices.

Is there calling or SMS?

No. Calling and SMS aren’t part of Smart Gurukul; people message each other in the app instead.
