FAQ

Parent app: common questions

Is there a separate app for parents?

No. Parents use the same Smart Gurukul app and sign in with a parent login. It shows their child’s attendance, fees and receipts, report card, timetable, upcoming tests and ID card, the school bus if the school uses it, and chat with teachers.

Can one parent see more than one child?

Yes. All of a parent’s children at the school appear on one login, and the parent can switch between them.

How do parents sign in?

If the school has the parent’s mobile number on the child’s record, the parent signs in with a one-time code sent on WhatsApp, and every child on that number appears. Other parents register with their child’s registration number and the school approves them.

Can parents chat with teachers?

Yes. Parents can chat with their child’s teachers and the school office in the app, and read the school’s announcements.

Will I be told if my child is absent?

Yes. When the class teacher saves the register with your child marked absent, you get a notification on your phone.

Can parents pay fees in the app?

Not yet. Parents see their bills, dues and receipts in the app and get reminders, and fees are paid at the school as today. Each payment then shows in the app with its receipt.

Can parents track the school bus?

Yes, if the school runs its buses on Smart Gurukul. Parents see the bus live on a map while their child is on it, and get an app notification and a WhatsApp message when their child boards, reaches school and is dropped off.
