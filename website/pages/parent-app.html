FAQ

Parent app: common questions

Is there a separate app for parents?

No, parents use the same Smart Gurukul app and simply sign in with a parent login. It shows only what a parent needs: their children’s attendance, fees and report cards.

Can one parent account see more than one child?

Yes. All of a parent’s children at the school appear on one login.

How do parents sign in?

With their mobile number and a one-time code. A parent first links to their child using details the school already holds, and the school approves the request.

Can parents pay fees in the app?

Parents can see fee dues and receipts today. Fully online fee payment through a payment gateway is coming soon.
