Two ways to sign in

Both start from the mobile number the school has on the child’s record.

  1. A one-time code on WhatsApp. Choose the school in the app, enter that number and tap “Send OTP”, then type the code that arrives on WhatsApp. You are signed in on your child’s own account. If more than one child at the school has that number on their record, the app asks “Who’s using the app?”, and “Switch child” in your profile moves between them later.
  2. A parent account. Tap “New here? Create an account”, then “I’m a parent”. Fill in “Child’s registration number” and “Your contact number on file”, which must be the number on the child’s record, then choose a username and password. Once the school approves the account, sign in with “Sign in with username & password instead”. A parent account covers one child, and adds absence alerts, the list of school announcements and chat with the child’s teachers and the school office.

For the school

Fewer calls to the office

When parents can check attendance, dues, receipts and results themselves, the office spends less time answering the same questions on the phone. Signing in starts from the mobile number the school already has on the child’s record: a one-time code sent to it on WhatsApp opens the child’s account, or the parent uses it, with the child’s registration number, to create a parent account, which the school approves.

Are you a parent?

Smart Gurukul is set up by your child’s school. Once your mobile number is on your child’s record there, you can sign in. If your school doesn’t use it yet, share this page with them.

FAQ

Parent app: common questions

Is there a separate app for parents?

No. Parents use the same Smart Gurukul app. It shows their child’s attendance, fees and receipts, report card, timetable, upcoming tests and ID card, and the school bus if the school uses it. A parent account adds absence alerts, the school’s announcements and chat with the child’s teachers and the school office.

Can one parent see more than one child?

Yes, with the WhatsApp code: every child at the school whose record has that mobile number appears, and the parent switches between them in the app. A parent account created in the app covers one child.

How do parents sign in?

There are two ways, and both use the mobile number the school has on the child’s record. With a one-time code sent on WhatsApp, the parent signs in on the child’s own account and can switch between all the children on that number. Or the parent creates a parent account with the child’s registration number and that mobile number, and signs in with a username and password once the school approves it. A parent account adds absence alerts, the school’s announcements and chat with the child’s teachers and the school office.

Can parents chat with teachers?

Yes, with a parent account. Parents can chat with their child’s teachers and the school office in the app, and read the school’s announcements.

Will I be told if my child is absent?

Yes, with a parent account. When the class teacher saves the register with your child marked absent, you get a notification on your phone. Absence alerts go to parent accounts, not to the child’s own account that the WhatsApp code signs in to.

Can parents pay fees in the app?

Not yet. Parents see their bills, dues and receipts in the app and get reminders, and fees are paid at the school as today. Each payment then shows in the app with its receipt.

Can parents track the school bus?

Yes, if the school runs its buses on Smart Gurukul. Parents see the bus live on a map while their child is on it, and get an app notification and a WhatsApp message when their child boards, reaches school and is dropped off.
