What is Smart Gurukul?

Smart Gurukul is an AI-powered, gamified school app for Indian K-12 schools. It puts school operations (GPS attendance, fees, payroll, exams and report cards, chat and video calls) in the same app as an AI helpdesk and teaching helper, and as game-based learning with XP, streaks, leagues, House Wars and live quiz battles. Principals, teachers, students and parents each get their own view. It works in English and Hindi and needs no attendance hardware.

FAQ

Questions schools ask us

What is Smart Gurukul?

Smart Gurukul is an AI-powered, gamified school app for Indian K-12 schools. It combines school management (attendance, fees, payroll, exams, report cards, communication) with an AI helpdesk and teaching helper and with game-based learning (XP, streaks, leagues, House Wars and quiz battles), in one app for principals, teachers, students and parents.

How is Smart Gurukul different from other school ERP software?

Most school ERPs are record-keeping tools for the office. Smart Gurukul also has built-in AI that answers questions from your school’s own data, GPS-geofenced staff attendance that needs no biometric machine, and a full gamification layer that gives students a reason to use the app every day.

Does GPS attendance need any hardware?

No. Staff mark their own attendance from their phone, and the app only accepts it when they are inside your school’s geofence. If you already have RFID, fingerprint or face devices, they can connect through our attendance device API; that integration is set up per school.

Does the app work in Hindi?

Yes. The app can be switched between English and Hindi, and the AI assistants reply in English, Hindi or Hinglish, matching the language of the question.

Which boards and school types is it for?

Smart Gurukul is built for private K-12 schools in India, including CBSE and State Board schools and school groups. Grading scales and exam terms are configurable, so it fits your board’s pattern.

Do parents get their own app?

Yes. Parents use the same Smart Gurukul app with a parent login. One login covers all their children, with daily attendance and a monthly calendar, fee dues and receipts, and published report cards. Parent–teacher messaging is coming soon.

How long does setup take, and do we have to enter data ourselves?

You don’t retype anything. Share the Excel registers you already have and our team imports your students, classes and staff. Teachers and parents then join with invite codes and one-time login codes.

How much does Smart Gurukul cost?

Pricing is per student per year and depends on your school’s size. We share a quote after the demo, and every school can start with a 1-month pilot before committing.

Is our school’s data safe?

Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to the user’s phone, the app talks to our servers over encrypted HTTPS, each school’s records are kept separate, and an activity log shows who changed what. Read our privacy policy.

Is it available on Android and iPhone?

Smart Gurukul is an Android app today, with the public Google Play listing coming soon; schools get access during onboarding. iPhone support is in testing.
