A school day in five steps

1

A teacher checks in by GPS at the school gate

2

The class teacher marks attendance in seconds

3

A subject teacher enters marks; the report card publishes

4

A parent sees it and messages the teacher

5

The principal exports the month's attendance to Excel

Now let's see it in the app.

Switch to the app here. Demo script: (1) Mark My Attendance on a teacher login. (2) My Class tile, take attendance. (3) Assessment, enter marks, Publish Report Cards. (4) Parent login, Report Card, then Chat. (5) Principal home, Reports, export the month's attendance and share the sheet. Then open Profile to show the digital ID card and download it. Finish on the Activity Log to show every change is recorded.
