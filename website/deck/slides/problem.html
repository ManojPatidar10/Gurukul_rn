Problem

Most schools still run on paper registers, spreadsheets and WhatsApp

No single source of truth

Attendance and fee dues are scattered across registers and spreadsheets.

Report cards made by hand

Assembled every term from marksheets kept by each teacher.

Payroll is manual

Salaries worked out by hand every month, with errors that are hard to trace.

Parent communication leaves no trail

Phone calls and unofficial WhatsApp groups, with nothing on record.

Existing school ERPs cover records and fees, but are weak on real-time communication and need purchased hardware for attendance.

Ask the school which of these four they feel most today, and come back to it during the demo.
