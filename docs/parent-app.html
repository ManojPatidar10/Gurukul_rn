FAQ

Parent app: common questions

Is there a separate app for parents?

No. Parents use the same Smart Gurukul app and sign in with a parent login. It shows their child’s attendance, fees and report card.

Can one parent see more than one child?

Yes. All of a parent’s children at the school appear on one login.

How do parents sign in?

With their mobile number and a one-time code. A parent first links to their child using details the school already has, and the school approves the request.

Can parents chat with teachers or pay fees in the app?

Not yet. Parent chat, parent alerts and online fee payment are coming soon. Today parents can see fees and dues in the app.
