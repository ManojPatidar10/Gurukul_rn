Looking for a school ERP? Here’s how Smart Gurukul is different.

A traditional school ERP is a record-keeping system for the office. Smart Gurukul covers the core records too, then adds AI, phone-based GPS attendance and a game layer that brings teachers, students and parents into the app every day.

In short: choose Smart Gurukul if you want the whole school to actually use the app, not just the office. It needs no attendance hardware, has AI built in, works in Hindi and English, and gamifies attendance and learning. Choose a large ERP today if you need transport, hostel, library or statutory payroll (PF/ESI/TDS) modules immediately; those are not in Smart Gurukul yet.

FAQ

Choosing school software

What is the difference between a school ERP and Smart Gurukul?

A school ERP is mainly an office record-keeping system. Smart Gurukul covers the core records (students, staff, attendance, fees, payroll, exams, report cards) and adds an AI Helpdesk and Academic Helper, GPS attendance that needs no hardware, and gamification (XP, streaks, leagues, House Wars and quiz battles) so teachers, students and parents use it daily.

Is Smart Gurukul a good ERP alternative for a small or mid-sized private school?

Yes, it’s designed for private budget and mid-fee schools in India with a few hundred students or more, including CBSE and State Board schools and school groups. It needs no hardware and our team imports your existing data.

What does Smart Gurukul not do yet?

Transport and bus tracking, hostel, library, inventory, timetable, homework, SMS gateway, ID cards, admission enquiries, statutory payroll (PF/ESI/TDS) and online fee payment through a gateway are not available yet. Several are on our coming-soon roadmap.

Can we move from our current ERP to Smart Gurukul?

Yes. Export your students and staff to Excel, or share the registers you keep, and our team imports them for you. You can then run a 1-month pilot alongside your current system.
