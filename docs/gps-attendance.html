Teacher attendance by GPS, with no machine to buy

Teachers check in from their own phone when they reach school. There’s no biometric machine to buy, queue at or repair, and student attendance takes the class teacher two taps.

In short: the school saves its location and a radius once (100 metres by default). When a teacher taps Mark my attendance, the phone sends its GPS position and our server, not the phone, checks the distance to the school. At school, they’re marked present. Too far away, the check-in is refused and shows how far they are. Check-ins from phones with a fake-GPS app, Developer options, root or a cloner app are refused too.

How it works

How does GPS teacher attendance work?

  1. Save the school’s location. The principal taps “use my current location” at school and sets the radius.
  2. Teachers check in on arrival. One tap, and the phone shares its GPS position.
  3. The server decides. Distance is worked out on our server from the school’s saved location, so the phone can’t simply declare itself “at school”.
  4. History fills itself in. Every teacher gets a monthly calendar of present, absent, late and half-day.

FAQ

GPS attendance: common questions

How does GPS attendance work for teachers?

The school saves its location and a radius. A teacher taps “Mark my attendance” on arrival, the phone sends its GPS position, and our server checks the distance to the school. At school, they are marked present; too far away, the check-in is refused.

Can teachers fake their location?

The app refuses a check-in when the phone reports a fake (mock) location, has Developer options turned on, is rooted or jailbroken, or runs Smart Gurukul inside a cloner app. Our server also rejects stale locations and anything outside the school’s radius. These signals come from the phone, so no GPS system is as tamper-proof as a fingerprint machine, but the common fake-GPS tricks are blocked.

What radius should a school use?

The default is 100 metres from the saved point. Larger campuses can increase it, up to 700 metres.

What if a teacher’s check-in is refused?

The app says why. Turn off any fake-GPS app and Developer options, open Smart Gurukul directly rather than inside a cloner app, and try again at school. An admin can also mark a teacher’s attendance.

Do we need a biometric machine?

No. GPS check-in uses teachers’ own phones, so there is no hardware to buy or repair.
