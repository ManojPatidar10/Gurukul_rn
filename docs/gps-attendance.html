GPS attendance for schools, with no machine to buy

Teachers and staff mark themselves present from their own phone, but only when they are actually on campus. Class attendance takes a teacher a few taps. Everything lands in one record the principal and parents can see.

Book a free demo

In short: geofenced attendance means the school sets its location and a radius (100 metres by default). When a staff member taps Mark my attendance, the phone sends its GPS position and our server, not the phone, checks the distance to the school. Inside the radius, they’re marked present; outside, it’s refused and shows how far away they are.

Staff attendance

How geofenced check-in works

  1. Set the school’s location once. The principal taps “use my current location” on campus and sets the radius.
  2. Staff check in from their phone. One tap on arrival; the phone shares its GPS position.
  3. The server decides. Distance is calculated on our server from the school’s stored coordinates, so the phone can’t simply declare itself “inside”.
  4. History is automatic. Each person gets a monthly calendar of present, absent, late and half-day.

Connecting existing attendance devices

Schools that already use RFID cards, fingerprint or face-recognition devices don’t have to throw them away. Smart Gurukul has an attendance device API that devices can send events to; the hookup for a specific brand is set up per school during onboarding.

FAQ

GPS attendance: common questions

How does GPS attendance work for teachers?

The school sets its location and a radius. A teacher taps “Mark my attendance” on arrival, the phone sends its GPS position, and the server checks the distance to the school. Inside the radius they are marked present; outside, the check-in is refused.

Can staff fake their location?

The in/out decision is made on the server from the school’s stored coordinates, never by the phone, and the location and accuracy of every check-in are recorded. As with any GPS system, a determined person using special spoofing tools on their phone is harder to rule out than with a biometric device.

What radius should a school use?

The default is 100 metres from the point you set. Larger campuses can increase it from the school’s location settings.

Do we need a biometric machine?

No. GPS attendance uses staff phones. If you already have RFID, fingerprint or face devices, they can connect through our attendance device API.
