Attendance

Live student and staff attendance, class-wise breakdowns, and a full calendar history for every student, teacher, and parent.

Fees & Payroll

Fee structures, dues, and payments for parents to track — plus payroll and salary history for staff.

XP, streaks, leaderboards, and live quiz battles that make learning something students actually look forward to.

Parents get their own account to check attendance, fees, and report cards for each of their children — no extra paperwork.
