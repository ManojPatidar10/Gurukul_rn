What is Smart Gurukul?

Smart Gurukul is a school management app for Indian schools, with AI and games built in. It connects principals, teachers, students and parents in one app. School work (GPS teacher attendance, class attendance, fees and receipts, payroll and expenses, timetable and substitutions, exams and report cards, school bus tracking, chat, announcements and events) sits alongside AI tools (an AI helpdesk, Academic Helper and a question paper generator) and game-based learning with XP, streaks, leagues, House Wars and quiz battles. Everyone gets their own view, in English or Hindi, with no attendance hardware to buy.

FAQ

Questions schools ask us

What is Smart Gurukul?

Smart Gurukul is a school management app for Indian schools, with AI and games built in. It connects principals, teachers, students and parents in one app. School work (GPS teacher attendance, class attendance, fees and receipts, payroll and expenses, timetable and substitutions, exams and report cards, school bus tracking, chat, announcements and events) sits alongside AI tools (an AI helpdesk, Academic Helper and a question paper generator) and game-based learning with XP, streaks, leagues, House Wars and quiz battles. Everyone gets their own view, in English or Hindi, with no attendance hardware to buy.

How is Smart Gurukul different from other school management software?

Most school management software is a record-keeping tool for the office. Smart Gurukul covers the core records (students, staff, attendance, fees, payroll, exams, report cards and the timetable) and adds AI tools, GPS teacher attendance with no biometric machine, school bus tracking from the driver’s phone, and a game layer that gives students a reason to open the app every day.

Does GPS attendance need any hardware?

No. Teachers check in by GPS from their own phone when they are at school, and the check is done on our server. Student attendance is marked by the class teacher in the app.

Does the app work in Hindi?

Yes. Anyone can switch the app between Hindi and English from the language button at the top of the screen. The parent home and child dashboard, sign-in, fees, report cards, timetable, school bus and ID cards are in Hindi; a few screens, such as attendance history, the chat list and school events, are still in English.

Which schools is it for?

Private CBSE and State Board schools in India of any size. Each school, or each branch of a group, runs as its own Smart Gurukul account. Grading scales and exam terms can be set to match your school’s pattern.

What do parents see?

Parents use the same app, in Hindi or English. They see their child’s attendance, fees and receipts, report card, timetable and, if the school runs its buses on Smart Gurukul, the school bus, and get a reminder when a fee is due. A parent can sign in on the child’s account with a one-time code sent on WhatsApp to the number on the child’s record; every child whose record has that number shows up, and the parent switches between them. Or the parent can create a parent account, which the school approves. A parent account adds alerts when their child is marked absent, the school’s announcements and chat with their child’s teachers and the school office.

Do we have to enter all our data ourselves?

No. Send us the student register you already keep and we import it. We also train your staff. Teachers join with an invite code or a one-time code on WhatsApp, and parents with a one-time code or a parent account.

How much does it cost?

Every school starts with a free 1-month pilot. After the demo we share a quote based on your school’s size.

Is our school’s data safe?

People sign in with a one-time code sent on WhatsApp to the mobile number the school has on record, or with a username and password. We store passwords only as secure hashes, never as plain text. The app talks to our servers over encrypted HTTPS, each school’s records are kept separate, and an activity log shows who changed what. Read our privacy policy.
