What is Smart Gurukul?

Smart Gurukul is an AI-powered, gamified school app for Indian schools. It connects the whole school in one app. School work (GPS attendance, fees, payroll, exams and report cards, chat, video calls and events) sits alongside an AI helpdesk assistant and game-based learning with XP, streaks, leagues, House Wars and quiz battles. Principals, teachers, students and parents each get their own view, in English or Hindi, with no attendance hardware to buy.

FAQ

Questions schools ask us

What is Smart Gurukul?

Smart Gurukul is an AI-powered, gamified school app for Indian schools. It connects principals, teachers, students and parents in one app, with attendance, fees, payroll, exams, report cards and communication alongside an AI helpdesk assistant and game-based learning (XP, streaks, leagues, House Wars and quiz battles).

How is Smart Gurukul different from other school management software?

Most school software is a record-keeping tool for the office. Smart Gurukul also has an AI helpdesk assistant built in, GPS teacher attendance that needs no biometric machine, and a full game layer that gives students a reason to open the app every day.

Does GPS attendance need any hardware?

No. Teachers check in by GPS from their own phone when they are at school, and the check is done on our server. Student attendance is marked by the class teacher in the app.

Does the app work in Hindi?

Yes. The whole app can be used in English or Hindi.

Which schools is it for?

Private schools in India, including CBSE and State Board schools and school groups. Grading scales and exam terms can be set to match your school’s pattern.

What do parents see?

Parents use the same app with a parent login and see their child’s attendance, fees and report card, in Hindi or English. All their children appear on one login. Parents can also chat with their child’s teachers and get alerts for absences and fee due dates.

Do we have to enter all our data ourselves?

No. Send us the student register you already keep and we import it. We also train your staff, and teachers and parents join with invite codes and a one-time code on their phone.

How much does it cost?

Every school starts with a free 1-month pilot. After the demo we share a quote based on your school’s size.

Is our school’s data safe?

Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to the user’s phone, the app talks to our servers over encrypted HTTPS, each school’s records are kept separate, and an activity log shows who changed what. Read our privacy policy.

Who builds Smart Gurukul?

Smart Gurukul is built in India by an IIT-BHU team.
