Problem

Most schools still run on paper registers, spreadsheets and WhatsApp

No single source of truth

Attendance and fee dues are scattered across registers and spreadsheets.

Report cards made by hand

Assembled every term from marksheets kept by each teacher.

Payroll is manual

Salaries worked out by hand every month, with errors that are hard to trace.

Parent communication leaves no trail

Phone calls and unofficial WhatsApp groups, with nothing on record.

Existing school ERPs cover records and fees, but are weak on real-time communication and need purchased hardware for attendance.

Solution

One app and one login for everyone in the school

Principal

Students and staff, fees, payroll, report cards, approvals and a full activity log.

Teacher

GPS check-in, class attendance, marks entry and messages to parents.

Student

Attendance, marks and report card, plus quizzes, XP and House Wars.

Parent

Their child's attendance, fees and report card, and a direct line to teachers.

English and Hindi · Android and iOS · every school's data kept separate

Features

Everything your school runs on, in one app

Academics

Classes, timetables, assessments, marks entry, grading scale, report cards

Attendance

Daily class attendance, GPS staff check-in, RFID/biometric device API, Excel export

Fees and finance

Fee structures and dues, payment recording, fund and dues reports, expenses

Staff and payroll

Staff records, digital ID cards, salary structures, payroll, payslips

Communication

1:1 chat with parents and staff, announcements, push notifications

AI and engagement

AI helpdesk in English and Hindi, AI quiz generator, XP and House Wars

New this month

Simple for every role, private by design

A cleaner home screen

The principal sees 10 tiles, teachers and students 8. Everything else is grouped inside them.

Digital ID cards

Every student and staff member gets a professional ID card in the app, ready to download.

Any photo works

Upload straight from the phone's camera or gallery; the app resizes it for you.

Each role sees only its own data

Bank details, fees and marks are locked to the right people, and published report cards can't be changed.

Learn with fun

Students come back because learning feels like a game

XP and streaks

Points for attending and practising, and a streak to keep alive

Leagues

Climb from league to league with classmates each week

House Wars

Every student's effort earns points for their school house

Quiz battles

One-on-one challenges and live buzzer rounds on class subjects

Questions come from the school's own teachers, so the fun follows the syllabus.
