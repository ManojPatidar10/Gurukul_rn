FAQ

Choosing school software

How is Smart Gurukul different from other school management software?

Most school management software is an office record-keeping system. Smart Gurukul covers the core records (students, staff, attendance, fees, payroll, exams, report cards) and adds an AI helpdesk assistant, GPS teacher attendance that needs no hardware, and games (XP, streaks, leagues, House Wars and quiz battles) so teachers, students and parents use it daily.

Is Smart Gurukul a good fit for a small or mid-sized private school?

Yes. It’s made for private schools in India, including CBSE and State Board schools and school groups. It needs no hardware, our team imports your existing register, and you can try it free for a month.

What does Smart Gurukul not do yet?

Parent chat and alerts, online fee payment, timetable, homework, transport and bus tracking, SMS, ID cards and admissions are coming soon. Hostel, library and statutory payroll (PF/ESI/TDS) are not available yet.

Can we move from our current software to Smart Gurukul?

Yes. Export your students and staff to Excel, or share the register you keep, and our team imports it. You can run the free 1-month pilot alongside your current system.
